# Selection Rules

- Judge visible images, not keyword proximity.
- Require direction fit, mechanism, transfer value, source URL, image URL, and unique contribution.
- Reject duplicates, alternate crops, repeated mechanisms/values, keyword-only matches, broken sources, and poor usable quality.
- Keep exactly 10 positives per direction: 5 inspiration references and 5 execution references when enough qualified material exists.
- Use exactly four negative examples violating different project-specific criteria.
