# Strategy

Derive directions from standards, recent feedback, category baseline, and recurring mechanisms.

Each provisional direction records two-digit ID, name, definition, supporting insight/attribution IDs, mechanisms, expected perception, evidence strength, carrier-specific translation, and risk.

Each final direction records approved candidate ID, goal, first perception, 3–5 keywords, mechanism, carrier translation, and risk. Directions differ in at least two substantive dimensions such as goal, subject relationship, narrative, density, hierarchy, composition, or motion.
