# Research

Research in this order: carrier boundary, local standards, adaptive query planning, feedback and case/visual-observation collection, category baseline, insights, visual attribution, provisional directions.

Treat Xiaohongshu, Weibo, and Voice of User as multi-evidence search surfaces. On each relevant accessible platform, run two independent tracks for each carrier:

- `用户反馈`: use natural user language for confusion, interruption, mistrust, navigation, benefit comprehension, mis-touch, control, fatigue, and other observable experiences.
- `案例/视觉观察`: use design-publisher language such as campaign design, shopping-festival visual, marketing visual, venue design, splash design, motion visual, brand design, project review, and design review.

Create query families from four optional dimensions: carrier, evidence type, semantic facet, and project anchor. A project anchor such as `双十一`, `618`, campaign year, or brand is optional and must not appear in every query. Start with both anchored and unanchored variants.

Adapt after every result round:

1. When results are empty, remove the project anchor or professional carrier term and search the underlying user behavior, problem, campaign function, or design vocabulary.
2. When results are too broad, add carrier signals or a relevant semantic facet.
3. When an opened detail page is relevant, extract its native wording, hashtags, and adjacent concepts for the next round.
4. When feedback dominates, switch to design-publisher vocabulary; when design posts dominate, switch to user-language problem vocabulary.
5. Stop a query family after repeated empty or irrelevant results. Record the shortfall instead of mechanically paginating.

Feedback records original excerpt, visible date, source URL, audience type when known, surface feedback, deeper reason, carrier, and confidence. Reject undated, duplicated, older-than-24-month, irrelevant, or ordinary product-review items.

Every counted feedback item from Xiaohongshu, Weibo, or Voice of User requires a genuine screenshot captured from its opened original detail page. Capture the whole relevant post container, including platform context, author/account context, full relevant text, all evidence-bearing media, visible date, and relevant comments when they support the interpretation. When the post does not fit one viewport, use an element screenshot, stitched capture, or full-page capture that preserves the complete post. Do not substitute a search-result card, thumbnail, quote crop, HTML recreation, or isolated downloaded image.

Visual observations record source, date when visible, carrier, complete evidence, observable mechanism, solved problem, transfer value, and limitation. They are not competitor cases and carry no group quota.

Design posts may be recorded as `design_cases` and used as visual observations after the original detail page is opened and the carrier is verified. Do not rank brands or turn them into a competitor-case module. Classify every candidate as `feedback`, `design_case`, `visual_observation`, or `rejected`; one platform may supply all four outcomes.

Every counted social-platform `design_case` also requires a complete original-detail screenshot with its publisher context, full relevant description, complete evidence-bearing media, visible date when shown, and relevant comments when used. Preserve the whole in-scope carrier asset with uncropped treatment. A media download may supplement the screenshot for visual inspection but cannot replace source-context evidence.

Pair each screenshot with its exact quote or observable fact, design interpretation, project implication, platform, detail URL, visible date, audience or publisher type, engagement signals when visible, and confidence. Keep these elements in the same evidence card. If capture coverage is incomplete or the source context cannot be verified, classify the item as `rejected` or a limitation and do not count or display it as complete evidence.

Prefer two evidence types for important claims. Downgrade single-source claims. Keep fact, user statement, and inference distinct.
