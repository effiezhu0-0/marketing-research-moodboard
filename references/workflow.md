# Workflow

This is a mandatory sequential workflow. Complete and verify each numbered step before starting the next. Do not skip, merge, reorder, or silently substitute sources.

1. Parse the brief and use exactly three directions by default.
2. Define each carrier's inclusion, exclusion, and good criteria.
3. Check Chrome login state.
4. Build carrier-separated query families for both `用户反馈` and `案例/视觉观察`. Include anchored and unanchored variants; never require the project keyword in every query.
5. Enter only permitted platforms directly. Run both evidence tracks on every relevant accessible first-stage platform, adapt queries from result quality and platform-native wording, and open original detail pages. Never use a general-purpose search engine.
6. After copyright acknowledgement, capture every qualified feedback item and social-platform design case from its original detail page. Preserve the complete post context, author/account, full relevant text, evidence-bearing media, visible date, and relevant supporting comments; use element, stitched, or full-page capture when necessary. Do not use search thumbnails or reconstructed post cards.
7. Classify every opened candidate as feedback, design case, visual observation, or rejected; verify screenshot completeness and record the query family, adaptation reason, screenshot path, capture coverage, and outcome.
8. Build carrier-distinct baselines, insights, attribution, and exactly three provisional directions in the analysis and structured data. Retain a carrier tag on every evidence card.
9. Present the three provisional directions and evidence summary in the conversation; stop for explicit approval without creating a separate research HTML or PNG.
10. After approval, search Pinterest, Behance, and ZCOOL by direction.
11. Remove duplicates, lock ten qualified references per direction when available, then download the acknowledged references. Disclose genuine shortfalls instead of padding.
12. Select four negatives, compose the single combined `视觉趋势 + 视觉参考` HTML, render, and audit it.
