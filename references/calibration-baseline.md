# 工作流强制规则

Read completely at every invocation. These are mandatory constraints, not recommendations.

- Follow `references/workflow.md` in its numbered order. Never skip, merge, reorder, or start a later step before the current step is complete.
- Never replace a required or permitted source with an unapproved source. Record access and evidence limitations instead.
- Never use Google, Baidu, Bing, or another general-purpose search engine as an entry point, discovery method, observation surface, or evidence source.
- Enter permitted platforms directly, use on-site navigation or search, and open original detail/source pages before counting or interpreting material.
- Limit scope to e-commerce promotion venues and splash advertisements.
- Separate carriers before searching; never use one carrier to satisfy the other.
- Exclude all interaction/gameplay sections and ordinary App-interface research.
- Use the local carrier standard to decide what the carrier is and what good execution means.
- Do not create competitor groups, quotas, rankings, or winner recommendations.
- Use public visual material only as traceable observations for baselines and recurring mechanisms.
- Count or quote only user feedback with a visible date inside the exact rolling 24-month window.
- Present exactly three provisional directions with evidence in the conversation and obtain approval before visual-reference collection. Do not create a separate research HTML or PNG by default.
- After approval, search Pinterest, Behance, and ZCOOL by direction.
- Target 10 qualified positive references per direction and four negative examples; disclose genuine shortfalls instead of padding, as required by SKILL.md.
- Split each direction's positive references into exactly 5 inspiration references and 5 execution references when enough qualified material exists.
- Screen without downloads; download only locked selections after copyright acknowledgement.
- Treat every invocation as a clean evidence set.
